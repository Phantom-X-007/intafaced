THE BALANCE

Money is never trapped in a room. Trade profit is instantly spendable on the card, stakeable in yield, or collateral on a loan. No transfers. No waiting. A merchant's settlement lands in the same account they trade from. Come in any door; the money works in all of them.

Under it — the ledger is law:

Double-entry, always. Every transaction sums to zero per asset, in one serializable database transaction. Σ debits = Σ credits, or it doesn't post. No module holds a balance. Not for fees, not for rewards, not for gas. One wallet graph for the entire OS. 18 decimals of precision. Money is never a floating-point number. Hash-chained. Every transaction hashes the one before it. A tamper-evident book. Reconciled hourly. Snapshots must match a full entry replay to 18 decimals. A mismatch pages an operator and freezes the module that diverged. Recipes only. Deposits, fills, escrow, stake, collateral, liquidation, fees, rewards — approved recipes, never invented balances. No raw database writes from any service. Ever.

THE TOKEN

One asset carrying value between every room, across both planes. Pay any fee in it for a standing discount. Every reward paid in it. A fixed slice of every module's revenue buys it back.
