I · The manifesto

Every financial platform before this one was built by suits, for suits.

Cold interfaces. Corporate language. Gatekeeping dressed up as compliance. The culture — traders, hustlers, crews, people moving money at 3am across six currencies — got filed as a risk category instead of the customer base.

INTAFACED flips the table. Exchange. Broker. Bank. Payments. Launchpad. Prediction markets. Marketplace. Academy. Plus our own Layer 1 underneath all of it. Engineered with the energy of a AAA title instead of a quarterly report.

The interface feels like a lobby, not a spreadsheet. Progression feels like ranking up, not filling forms. The academy is a world you walk into, not a PDF graveyard. The token is mined, earned, staked, spent and burned inside one closed economy.

Black glass and bright green. Poster type and a tagger's hand. Nothing here looks like a bank — that is the entire point. Virality is not a marketing budget. It's a design decision. People share what looks like them.

II · Two planes. One economy.

Everyone made you choose. We refuse the trade.

Web2 gave you rails and kept the keys. Web3 gave you keys and took away the rails. We built both — and drew the line between them in code.

The Fiat Plane — we hold it, so we say so

Custodial. Fully compliant. Trade, bank, cards, fiat processing, institutional flow.

  • Tiered verification per jurisdiction — geo rules are config, not code
  • Every movement is a double-entry ledger transaction. No exceptions.
  • Three-layer custody: cold, warm, hot — multi-sig approval workflow
  • Connects to money as the world currently runs it — responsibility in writing

The Protocol Plane — you hold it, so there's nothing to ask

Genuinely non-custodial. Zero KYC — not a loophole, an architectural fact.

  • Self-custody smart accounts — passkey native, no seed phrase
  • On-chain order books, contract-held collateral
  • Session keys you grant and revoke — never withdrawal rights
  • Optional stealth handles: one human, two unlinkable presentations
ZERO-KYC FOLLOWS CUSTODY. EVERYWHERE. WITHOUT EXCEPTION. If we never hold it → permissionless. If we hold it → Fiat Plane with tiered verification, stated on screen. custody-scan fails the build if Protocol services write our ledger or contracts grant us withdrawal power. PROVABLY NON-CUSTODIAL, OR IT DOESN'T MERGE.

VI · Three shared systems

If a feature needs a fourth, the design is wrong.

The Identity

Verify once. Trade, bank, launch, learn, sell, predict — everywhere. No repeat paperwork. Academy certs cut trading fees. Clean P2P raises card limits. Rank earned in any room counts in every room.

The Balance

Money is never trapped in a room. Trade profit is spendable on the card, stakeable in yield, or collateral on a loan — no transfers, no waiting. Double-entry always. No module holds a balance. 18 decimals. Hash-chained. Recipes only — never invented balances.

The Token

One asset across rooms and planes. Fee discounts. Rewards paid in it. A fixed slice of revenue buys it back. Competitors can copy a room. They cannot copy the house — or the ground.

XXIII · What we will never do

Hard rules. Not slogans.

  • Never hold a balance outside the ledger. Not for fees. Not for rewards. Not for gas.
  • Never store money in a floating-point number. Exact, or it doesn't post.
  • Never ship custodial dressed as decentralised. Custody boundary is a build check.
  • Never call something done because it looks done.
  • Never dress a roadmap as a release. If it isn't live, the screen says so.
  • Never trade our alpha against you. House desk is a sealed tenant.
  • Never sell you, your data, or your identity graph.
  • Never depend on a partner to function. Every external rail is an adapter.
  • Never make you the product. You're the account holder.
